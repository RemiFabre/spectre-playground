the Spectre

In 2023 a single 13-sided shape was found that tiles the plane — but only aperiodically: the pattern never repeats. This is its equilateral form, with curved edges so that a bump only ever nests into a dent. That is the whole matching rule, and it is why some placements that look fine will still paint you into a corner. Smith, Myers, Kaplan & Goodman-Strauss →
